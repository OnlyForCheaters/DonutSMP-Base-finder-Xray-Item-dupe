ESP: Loads ore locations from a file and draws colored boxes around selected ores. You can change the range, box style, and ore filters. The PANIC button disables Keybindstester features until restart.
Seed Calculator: Takes a world seed and version, then estimates ore locations for an overlay. The panel warns that predictions may be imperfect.
DonutXMP: Scans loaded chunks as you move and highlights selected ores through walls.
Bow and Wind: Bow Boost has an arrow damage setting; Wind Boost extends upward movement after a wind charge launch.
Fun: Freecam, optional hidden hand, hostile mob glow ESP, and player glow ESP. These have configurable toggle keys.
Search: Highlights matching blocks and items in loaded chunks, such as anything with “diamond” in its name.
AH Sell: Repeats a chosen chat message or command on a timer. Its Command Searcher can repeat a command until it finds a block matching your search term.
Misc: Disable item cooldowns, make a client-side ghost copy of the held item, automatically restore ghost items, and clear them.
